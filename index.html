About P2PI

Connecting People, Not Just Devices.

P2PI is a peer-to-peer app that lets nearby phones talk to each other directly — chat, call and share — over Bluetooth. No SIM, no Wi-Fi, no servers, and no internet required.

What is P2PI?

Removing the middleman from local communication

Every day, millions of people are surrounded by others — in classrooms, buses, mosques, markets and neighbourhoods — yet they can only reach each other through a distant tower, a paid SIM, or an internet connection that isn't always there.

P2PI removes the middleman. Your phone connects directly to the phones around you using Bluetooth Low Energy, forming a small local mesh. You can message, voice-call and send photos to nearby people even when the network is down, the balance is zero, or there is no signal at all.

It's private by design — your conversations travel straight from one device to another and never touch a company's server.

Privacy Policy

p2pi has no central chat servers and collects no messages. Everything stays on your device or travels directly between nearby phones over Bluetooth.

The Short Version

Your chats, calls, voice notes and photos travel directly between nearby phones over Bluetooth and are never sent to our servers. To help messages reach devices just out of direct range, P2PI may relay another nearby user’s encrypted packet onward (a “mesh” hop). Relayed content is not read, stored, or copied off your device beyond passing it along.

Permissions and Why They’re Used

PermissionWhy P2PI asks for it
Bluetooth & Nearby DevicesTo discover and connect to nearby phones — the core of the offline mesh.
MicrophoneOnly for voice messages and voice/video calls you start.
Camera & PhotosOnly for video calls, sending photos, or choosing your profile picture.
Location (older Android)Required by older Android OS versions to scan for Bluetooth LE devices. P2PI never collects your GPS location.
NotificationsTo alert you about incoming nearby messages and calls.

Deleting Your Data

Your chat history, display name, and profile live only on your device. Clearing storage or uninstalling P2PI removes everything immediately. For inquiries, contact support@p2pi.net.
